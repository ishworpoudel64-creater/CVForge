Who I Am

I'm Ishwor Poudel, a Computer Engineering-focused student and self-taught developer from Nepal. I recently completed Class 12 with a GPA of approximately 3.6, and since then I've been deepening my programming skills through consistent, hands-on practice rather than formal coursework alone.

My interest in technology started with curiosity about how software actually works — from the logic behind a web page to the systems that power modern applications. That curiosity has grown into a genuine focus: I spend my time writing code, building small projects, and studying the fundamentals of full-stack development, databases, and artificial intelligence.

I'm currently working with Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS and SQL, and exploring backend frameworks like FastAPI and Flask alongside frontend fundamentals. I'm also experimenting with Three.js to understand how 3D experiences are built on the web. My long-term goal is to study Computer Engineering or Computer Science at a strong international university and grow into a highly skilled software engineer who builds useful, real-world technology.
